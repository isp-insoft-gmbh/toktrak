# MISSION

Deliver a simple, trustworthy internal workflow that turns developers' local
coding-harness usage into understandable projected-cost trends.

AI coding subscriptions obscure their effective consumption through heavily
subsidized flat pricing. TokTrak makes that usage visible enough for team cost
awareness without pretending to provide complete billing data or monitoring work
content.

Success means a developer installs one user-scoped tracker, usage uploads daily
without attention, and the team can inspect current totals, trends, averages,
leaderboards, and USD/EUR estimates in a live dashboard.
